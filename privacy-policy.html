Privacy Policy

CourtZ — Last updated: 20 August 2026

1. About CourtZ

CourtZ is an application for managing sports sessions, players, games and attendance. CourtZ is provided by the developer identified as CourtZ.

2. Information stored by CourtZ

CourtZ allows users to enter and store player information needed to operate the app. This information is limited to:

  • Player name
  • Sex (M/F)
  • Skill level

3. Where your information is stored

The information entered into CourtZ is stored locally on the device on which the app is installed. CourtZ does not upload this player information to CourtZ servers or transmit it to a third party.

4. How the information is used

The information is used only to provide the functionality of CourtZ, including managing players, sessions, games, attendance and related features.

5. Sharing of information

CourtZ does not sell, rent or share the player information stored in the app with third parties.

6. Accounts, advertising and analytics

CourtZ does not require users to create an account or sign in. CourtZ does not use advertising, analytics, tracking services or third-party services to collect or process the player information described in this policy.

7. Retention and deletion

Player information remains on the device until it is deleted by the user through the app or removed when the app's locally stored data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

CourtZ does not maintain a remote copy of this locally stored player information.

8. Security

Because the information is stored locally, protection of the information also depends on the security of the user's device. Users should use the device's available security features, such as a screen lock and current operating-system updates.

9. Children's privacy

CourtZ does not knowingly collect information from children through internet transmission or online accounts. All player information entered into the app remains locally on the user's device.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time if the functionality or data practices of CourtZ change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
