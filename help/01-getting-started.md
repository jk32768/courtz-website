\# Getting Started



Welcome to CourtZ.



CourtZ helps you organise court sports sessions by managing attendance, generating balanced games and keeping play running smoothly.



Whether you're organising a one-off session with friends or running a regular club session, CourtZ can adapt to the way you organise your sessions.



\## Free Trial



CourtZ includes a fully functional 30-day trial.



The first time you start CourtZ, you'll be asked for your club name and email address. This starts your trial and personalises your copy of CourtZ.



When you purchase CourtZ, you can activate your licence at any time from \*\*About\*\* on the More menu. Your club details, licence status and expiry date (where applicable) are displayed there.



\## Getting Started



CourtZ can be used in two ways.



\### Using Session Profiles (Recommended)



If you organise regular club sessions, Session Profiles are the recommended way to use CourtZ.



A Session Profile remembers the players and settings you regularly use, making it quick and easy to prepare each new session.



To get started, you'll first create your players, then create a Session Profile.



Continue to \*\*Players\*\* to begin.



\### Without a Session Profile



If you're organising a one-off session or don't need to save your setup, you can simply add players directly to the Waiting Room and begin generating games.



Players can be:



\- existing players from your master player list

\- guest players created for the current session



At the end of the session, guest players can be discarded or added to your master player list for future use.



Continue to \*\*Running a Session\*\* to learn more.



\## Which approach should I choose?



If you regularly organise the same group of players, we recommend using Session Profiles.



If you're organising an occasional session, you can work directly from the Waiting Room without creating a Session Profile.
