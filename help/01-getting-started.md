# Getting Started

Welcome to CourtZ.

CourtZ helps you organise court sports sessions by managing attendance, generating balanced games and keeping play running smoothly.

Whether you're organising a one-off session with friends or running a regular club session, CourtZ can adapt to the way you organise your sessions.

[Watch the six-minute getting-started tutorial](../index.html#tutorial) for a walkthrough of a four-court club session.

## Free Trial

CourtZ includes a fully functional 30-day trial.

The first time you start CourtZ, you'll be asked for your club name and email address. This starts your trial and personalises your copy of CourtZ.

When you purchase CourtZ, you can activate your licence at any time from **About** in the **Options** menu (⋮). Your club details, licence status and expiry date (where applicable) are also displayed there.

## Getting Started

Play Sessions can be started in two ways.

### Using Session Profiles (Recommended)

If you organise regular club sessions, **Session Profiles** are the recommended way to use CourtZ.

A [Session Profile](03-session-profiles.md) remembers the players and settings you regularly use, making it quick and easy to prepare each new session.

To get started:

1. Create your players.
2. Create a Session Profile.
3. Start your first session.

Continue to [Players](02-players.md) to begin.

### Without a Session Profile

If you're organising a one-off session or don't need to save your setup, you can simply add players directly to the Waiting Room and begin generating games.

Players can be:

- existing players from your master player list
- guest players created for the current session

At the end of the session, guest players can be discarded or added to your master player list for future use.

Continue to [Running a Session](04-running-a-session.md) to learn more.

### Running a Tournament

For a competition with scoring, draws and standings, create a **Tournament** from the **Sessions** tab. See [Tournament Mode](12-tournaments.md) for setup and scoring instructions.

### Adjusting a Round During Play

In a Play Session, you can swap players by dragging one player onto another or choosing **Swap player** from their menu. **On a phone or tablet, press and hold briefly before dragging**; swiping immediately scrolls the page. The blue **Undo** icon in the session selector strip reverses changes made during the current round. See [Manual Round Adjustments](05-game-generation.md#manual-round-adjustments).

## Which approach should I choose?

If you regularly organise the same group of players, we recommend using **Session Profiles**.

If you're organising an occasional session, you can work directly from the Waiting Room without creating a Session Profile.
