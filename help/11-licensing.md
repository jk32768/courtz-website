# Licensing

This page explains the CourtZ free trial, how to activate a licence and where to find your licence details.

---

## Free Trial

CourtZ includes a fully functional **30-day trial**.

When you start CourtZ for the first time, you'll be asked for your club name and email address. This starts your trial and personalises your copy of CourtZ.

When the trial expires, use the **Enter Licence Key** button on the Trial Expired screen to activate a licence.

---

## Activating a Licence

If you have a CourtZ licence key, you can activate it in either of these ways:

### From the Trial Expired screen

1. Open CourtZ.
2. Select **Enter Licence Key**.
3. Enter your licence key.
4. Select **Activate**.

CourtZ will validate the key and activate your licence if it is valid.

If the key is invalid or the licence has expired, CourtZ will display a message explaining the problem.

### From the About dialog

If you can access the main application:

1. Open the **More** menu.
2. Select **About**.
3. Use the licence activation option in the About dialog.
4. Enter your licence key and follow the prompts.

---

## Viewing Licence Details

Open **About** from the **More** menu to view information about your copy of CourtZ.

The About dialog displays:

* CourtZ version
* Club name
* Email address
* Licence status
* Licence type
* Issue date, where applicable
* Expiry date, where applicable
