# Licensing

This page explains the CourtZ free trial, how to request a licence key, how to activate your licence and where to find your licence details.

---

## Free Trial

CourtZ includes a fully functional **30-day trial**.

When you start CourtZ for the first time, you'll be asked for your club name and email address. This starts your trial and personalises your copy of CourtZ.

When your trial expires, you'll need a licence key to continue using CourtZ.

---

## Requesting a Licence Key

CourtZ licences are currently issued manually by email.

To request a licence key, email:

**[support@courtzapp.co.uk](mailto:support@courtzapp.co.uk)**

Please include:

* Your club name.
* The email address you registered with in CourtZ.

You'll receive a response by email with further information and, where appropriate, a licence key.

There is currently no online purchasing system.

---

## Activating a Licence

Once you receive your licence key, you can activate it directly in CourtZ.

### If your trial has expired

1. Open CourtZ.
2. Select **Enter Licence Key** on the Trial Expired screen.
3. Enter your licence key.
4. Select **Activate**.

CourtZ will validate the key and activate your licence if it is valid.

If the key is invalid or the licence has expired, CourtZ will display a message explaining the problem.

### If your trial is still active

You can also activate your licence before the trial expires:

1. Open the **Options** menu (⋮).
2. Select **About**.
3. Choose the licence activation option.
4. Enter your licence key and follow the prompts.

**Important:** The About screen is only accessible while you can access the main application. If your trial has expired, use **Enter Licence Key** on the Trial Expired screen instead.

---

## Viewing Licence Details

While you can access the main application, open **About** from the **Options** menu (⋮) to view information about your copy of CourtZ.

The About dialog displays:

* CourtZ version
* Club name
* Email address
* Licence status
* Licence type
* Issue date, where applicable
* Expiry date, where applicable
